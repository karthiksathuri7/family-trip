Queen of Hill Stations

Ooty Tourism

Ooty, also known as Udhagamandalam, is a popular hill station located in the Nilgiri Hills of Tamil Nadu, India. It is renowned for its scenic beauty, pleasant climate, and lush green landscapes.

Ooty is an ideal destination for families, couples, and nature lovers. Visitors can enjoy boat rides on Ooty Lake, explore the beautiful botanical gardens, take a ride on the Nilgiri Mountain Railway, and indulge in local cuisine.

Popular Places to Visit in Ooty

🌸 Ooty Lake

A serene lake where visitors can enjoy boating and relax amidst nature.

🌿 Botanical Gardens

A beautiful garden showcasing a variety of plants, flowers, and trees, perfect for a leisurely stroll.

🚂 Nilgiri Mountain Railway

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, this railway offers a scenic journey through the hills and valleys of Ooty.

🏞️ Doddabetta Peak

The highest peak in the Nilgiris, offering panoramic views of the surrounding hills and valleys.

Activities in Ooty

  • Boating on Ooty Lake
  • Exploring the Botanical Gardens
  • Riding the Nilgiri Mountain Railway
  • Trekking to Doddabetta Peak
  • Visiting tea plantations and factories
  • Shopping for local handicrafts and souvenirs
