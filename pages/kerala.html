God's Own Country

Kerala Tourism

Kerala, known as "God's Own Country", is a beautiful destination famous for its peaceful backwaters, lush green landscapes, beaches, hill stations and rich cultural heritage.

Kerala is an ideal destination for families, couples and nature lovers. Visitors can enjoy relaxing houseboat rides, explore beautiful hill stations, experience traditional Kerala culture and taste delicious local food.

Popular Places to Visit in Kerala

🌴 Alleppey

Famous for its beautiful backwaters and relaxing houseboat experiences.

🌿 Munnar

A peaceful hill station surrounded by tea plantations, mountains and pleasant weather.

🏖️ Kovalam

A popular beach destination known for its beautiful coastline and relaxing atmosphere.

🛶 Kumarakom

A peaceful backwater destination where visitors can enjoy nature and traditional boat rides.

Things to Do

  • 🛶 Enjoy a houseboat ride
  • 🌿 Explore tea plantations
  • 🏖️ Relax at beautiful beaches
  • 🍛 Try traditional Kerala food
  • 📸 Explore nature and local culture
